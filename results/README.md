# Simulation Results

## Overview

This project focuses on the numerical design and optical simulation of a bowl-shaped reflector for improving light collection and optical coupling performance.

The research workflow integrates mathematical modeling, Python-based numerical algorithms, ray-tracing simulation, and Ansys Zemax OpticStudio verification.

## Research Results

The complete research report includes:

Mathematical Modeling: Derivation and numerical implementation of the reflector geometry.

Python Algorithm Development: Numerical computation, reflector surface generation, and ray-tracing simulation.

Optical Simulation: Evaluation of light propagation and detector irradiance distributions using Ansys Zemax OpticStudio.

Performance Analysis: Analysis of simulated light collection efficiency and comparison of different optical configurations.

Engineering Evaluation: Discussion of modeling assumptions, simulation limitations, and potential design improvements.
